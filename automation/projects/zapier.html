About

Hi, I'm Ian.

I'm an AI Automation Specialist who builds systems in Make, n8n, Zapier, and GoHighLevel. I focus on the work that repeats every day: answering the same questions, moving data between tools, and following up with leads that went quiet. I use AI where it helps and plain logic where it doesn't, so every system is reliable and cheap to run.

Before automation, I spent eight years as a video editor. That work taught me to break big projects into small steps, catch the details that are slightly off, and deliver on deadline. I bring the same habits to every build: I learn how your process actually works, design the logic around it, and test the edge cases, like an invoice with no matching record or a call that comes in after hours, before anything goes live.

About

Hi, I'm Ian.

I'm a video editor with eight years of experience editing AI videos, UGC, ads, and podcasts for agencies and brands, including a stretch as Head of Production, leading recording, design, and client calls for a creative team.

Most of my recent work mixes AI with real footage. I build talking-head videos with HeyGen avatars and ElevenLabs voices, add AI B-roll to creator ads, and cut long podcast episodes into short clips for social. Much of it ran as paid ads, so every edit is built to grab attention early, hold it to the end, and work with the sound off.

I also build automations for small teams, and it shows in how I work: organized project files, clear handoffs, and on-time delivery.

What I Do

AI Videos
Avatar and cloned-voice videos that look and sound natural, from script to final cut.
UGC
Creator footage cut into ads that hold attention, with AI B-roll or product shots where they help.
Feed Ads

Podcasts
Full episodes edited clean, plus vertical clips for Reels, TikTok, and Shorts.
