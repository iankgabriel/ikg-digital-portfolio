Hi, I'm Ian.

I'm an AI Automation Specialist transitioning into this field from a different career, building on the belief that most small businesses lose real revenue to slow, manual processes that automation can fix in seconds. I design and ship workflows across Make, n8n, Zapier, and GoHighLevel — combining no-code platforms with AI models and Claude Code to handle lead response, document processing, and customer support without a human having to babysit every step.

I don't write code by hand day to day — I rely on Claude and Claude Code to handle the technical implementation, while I focus on understanding the actual business problem, designing the right logic, and making sure every automation I ship is something I can explain and defend, not just something that happens to work.
